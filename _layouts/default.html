Dr Aaron Smiles

Creative technologist and researcher with an EngD in computer vision. I prototype with generative video, 3D and sound, and I measure what the models keep and lose.

StillDrawn, my first benchmark, is pre-registered and checked against human raters: it measures whether an inked comic or manga panel still looks drawn once an image-to-video model animates it, and its data and code are public. I also release open-source tools, most recently loopcutter, which cuts sample-accurate loops for live DJ sets.

I hold an EngD in Computer Science from Queen Mary University of London, where I was part of The Centre for Advanced Robotics @ Queen Mary (ARQ) and the Human Augmentation & Interactive Robotics (HAIR) team. My research used stereo vision to detect and range transparent objects underwater, and produced an open dataset. My supervisors were Ildar Farkhatdinov and Changjae Oh.

I'm currently COO at Nuvelen, where we model how a forest grows and stores carbon, in effect a digital twin of each site that simulates years of growth from species growth curves, climate and soil data, checked against sensors in the ground. It is the question I bring to video and world models: how far can a simulated world drift from the real one before it stops being useful?

From 2011 to 2017 I ran a software startup building VR audio-visual production tools for the Oculus Rift DK1, where I worked on visual spatial sound rendering in virtual environments.

I also hold an MSc in Digital Media from Newcastle University, where I was part of Culture Lab and the parachuting club, and an Executive MBA from Quantic School of Business and Technology.
