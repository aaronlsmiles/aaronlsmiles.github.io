Aaron Smiles

I am an EngD student at The Centre for Advanced Robotics @ Queen Mary (ARQ), part of the Human Augmentation & Interactive Robotics (HAIR) team, at Queen Mary University London. My EngD supervisors are Ildar Farkhatdinov and Changjae Oh.

From 2011 to 2017, I ran a software startup developing VR audio-visual production tools for the Oculus Rift DK1, where I worked on visual spatial sound rendering in virtual environments.

I have an MRes in Digital Media (CS focus) from Newcastle University, where I worked in Culture Lab and was part of the paracuting club, and an Executive MBA from Quantic School of Business and Technology.
