Dr Aaron Smiles

I build evidence-led AI systems that turn messy real-world and environmental signals into decisions teams can trust.

My background spans applied AI, prompt/context engineering, and operations, with hands-on experience designing and shipping systems across research, product, and scale-up environments. I focus on making intelligence usable - grounding models in reality, stress-testing assumptions, and translating complexity into things that actually work day to day.

Currently operating at the intersection of AI, data, and delivery, quietly building the verification layer that modern systems depend on, whilst becoming ISO 27001 and 42001 Lead Implementer certified.

I hold an EngD in Applied 3D Computer Vision and AI, focused on environmental sensing, ground-truthing, and verification in complex natural systems, from The Centre for Advanced Robotics @ Queen Mary (ARQ), part of the Human Augmentation & Interactive Robotics (HAIR) team, at Queen Mary University London. My EngD supervisors were Ildar Farkhatdinov and Changjae Oh.

From 2011 to 2017, I ran a software startup developing VR audio-visual production tools for the Oculus Rift DK1, where I worked on visual spatial sound rendering in virtual environments.

I hold an MRes in Digital Media (CS focus) from Newcastle University, where I was part of Culture Lab and a member of the paracuting club, and I hold an Executive MBA from Quantic School of Business and Technology.
