A benchmark for one narrow question: when an image-to-video model animates an inked comic or manga panel, does the result still look drawn? InkBench pairs a 29-panel dataset (each panel with its line-art layer and generation recipe) with five automatic line-stability metrics and a pre-registered study of five video engines, two open-weights and three hosted. The pre-registered hypothesis returned null and is reported as registered. The failure taxonomy that emerged instead includes modes that stay in ink to a human eye while the metrics flag them, and modes the metrics barely see. A six-rater human check supports the metrics on content preservation; the absolute ink-register judgement proved weaker. An optical-flow control shows that engine rankings largely track how much each engine moves. Dataset, recipes and metrics are to be released with the preprint and are not yet public.
