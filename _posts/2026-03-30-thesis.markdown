This EngD thesis explores stereo vision-based object detection and ranging in underwater environments, with a focus on transparent objects such as plastic bottles. The research spans camera calibration for underwater use, YOLO-based detection pipelines, stereo depth estimation, and evaluation across multiple aquatic conditions. The work contributes a multi-environment stereo dataset, analysis of detection and ranging accuracy for transparent and opaque objects in air and water, and methods for augmented telepresence to assist robotic underwater waste collection.
