Working title. Video-generation engines are updated and retired continually, so a benchmark run once measures a single moment. This study would re-run StillDrawn's frozen protocol on the then-current engines at least six months after its release, to measure how far scores and rankings drift while the test itself stays fixed. It cannot start until StillDrawn is public, and its timing is TBA.
