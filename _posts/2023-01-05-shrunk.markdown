Stereopsis is important in virtual reality for natural depth perception. If the stereo baseline is unnatural it will induce headaches and simulator sickness.
  
An exciting use case of telexistence is being able to control tiny robotic avatars, reminiscent of <i>Honey, I Shrunk the Kids</i>.
    
The human pupillary distance is average 63mm: <i>How can we scale human stereopsis and depth perception proportionately for human operator vision to be the perspective eyes of a micro-, or nano-scale robot?</i>
