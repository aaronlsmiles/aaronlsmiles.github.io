An original science-fantasy universe, built out from design work I did in my teens — rebuilt from the story material rather than restored, with the original art kept as raw reference rather than treated as canon.
  
Concept art is produced through a directed generative pipeline — local diffusion alongside hosted models — working in a black-and-white ink substrate. Each round is judged against criteria frozen before generation and logged in a findings loop, so the visual direction moves on evidence rather than on taste.
  
In development. Storyline and design documents are not public.
