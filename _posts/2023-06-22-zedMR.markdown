3D computer vision and 3D AI-generated content (AIGC) research in Unity with Croquet and NVIDIA Omniverse using a Zed Mini with a VR HMD investigating interoperable multi-agent collaborative robot (cobot) scenarios. The goal of the research is to explore various human-robot interaction (HRI), or human-remote robot-robot interaction, scenarios using mixed reality, NLP, and AIGC. Such scenarios include:

i) Using two simulated robotic end effectors to perform assited grasping experiments.
  ii) AIGC mixed reality cues for cobot intent.
  iii) Bi-directional mixed reality cobot signals: We instruct robot, robot instructs us.
