Working title. Artists often draw many quick candidates and then commit to one. This work asks how that practice carries over to generative line art, and what is gained or lost when many generated candidates are consolidated into a single drawing. An early draft exists. Scope, venue and timing are to be announced, and the material may be folded into another paper rather than published on its own.
