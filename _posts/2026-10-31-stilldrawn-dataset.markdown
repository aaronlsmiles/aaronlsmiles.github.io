The data behind the StillDrawn benchmark: 29 inked comic and manga panels controlled for content and ink treatment, plus 5 ground-value variants, each with its line-art layer and generation recipe; all 373 clips generated from them by six image-to-video engines under a pre-registered protocol, with a per-clip manifest and metric outputs; and the frame strips and pseudonymised human ratings behind the paper's findings. Released under CC BY 4.0 alongside the preprint, with MIT-licensed code. Not yet public.
